## Direction contract

THESIS: Confident stillness, still — but now spent sparingly. Most of the page breathes in warm white and open space; the dark, weighted voice is saved for one closing moment instead of carrying the whole page.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white ground (`cream`) alternating with a soft blush tint (`cream-tint`) for most sections; one dark `ink` band reserved for the closing CTA. One oxblood accent throughout (buttons, emphasis, numeral badges), now on full-pill controls rather than hard edges. Bodoni Moda for headlines, Public Sans for body — unchanged.

STORY: A visitor lands on a light, spacious, centered page that reads as premium and unhurried (Apple-scale whitespace, Canva-soft section tinting and pill controls), moves through the philosophy and process at an easy pace, and arrives at a single dark, weighted closing section that asks them to book — the one place the page raises its voice.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky frosted header (wordmark + pill CTA, visible at every breakpoint including mobile) above a centered, warm-white hero: oversized centered Bodoni Moda headline, one-line positioning statement, single oxblood pill CTA. No eyebrow label, no stock photography, no gradient text.

FORM: User-directed revision — explicit request to blend Canva's approachable structure (pill buttons, soft tinted sections, rounded accent badges) with Apple's spacious restraint (centered columns, heavy vertical rhythm), while preserving Dr. Aamir's original confident-editorial identity rather than replacing it. Resolved by demoting the original all-dark system to a single closing band and opening the rest of the page onto a light, spacious canvas. Also closes a real gap: the previous header hid its CTA below `sm:`, leaving mobile with no functional header action — now fixed, CTA visible at every breakpoint.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
